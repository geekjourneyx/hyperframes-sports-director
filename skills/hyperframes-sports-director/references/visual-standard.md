# Visual Standard

Read this reference while defining the meaning inventory, visual worlds, and
asset roles, or while reviewing whether a generated asset belongs in a sports
Vlog. It states the shared HyperFrames visual standard; sport profiles adjust
policy thresholds without forking the pipeline.

## Meaning and visual worlds

Start with what the edit must communicate: departure, place, direction,
effort, change, pause, arrival, or return. Group related meanings into a small
visual world with one material language, lighting direction, grain, edge
treatment, spatial logic, and semantic-token palette. The full-resolution Style
Anchor proves that world before related component sheets are produced.

Retain the HyperFrames fallback grammar when the selected direction does not
override it: near-black ground, white/gray hierarchy, one restrained warm
accent, negative space, subtle grain, shallow depth, haze, rim light, and local
metallic highlights. Use only semantic color tokens from the frozen design
system; an asset-local hex color is not a token.

## Narrative roles

- `journey_anchor` locates or structures the journey: terrain contour, chapter
  place, privacy-trimmed route, pool length, destination, or return marker.
- `activity_evidence` presents truthful recorded or activity-derived evidence:
  a permitted effort interval, distance chapter, elevation segment, lap fact,
  or coverage-qualified metric. It never invents values and never uses raw GPS.
- `experience_carrier` carries sensation or spatial continuity: water flow,
  spray, wind, dust, mineral texture, shadow, environmental rhythm, or a
  restrained transition bridge.

An asset can have one narrative role. Role labels do not convert generated
media into evidence. Generated scenery, terrain, water, weather, landmarks, or
athlete imagery is always interpretive or decorative; it is prohibited as
documentary evidence and cannot impersonate an unrecorded shot.

## Composition and readability

Real footage remains visually primary. Keep generated layers sparse, readable,
and subordinate except at an approved interpretive hinge. Verify silhouette,
negative space, safe-zone containment, and density in real-footage combinations,
not only on isolated proof backgrounds.

For SDR Rec.709 output, critical text targets 7:1 local contrast and never falls
below 4.5:1; ordinary text remains at least 4.5:1; large text and meaningful
graphics remain at least 3:1. Sample moving intervals and preserve semantic
token colors within Delta E 2000 ≤3 after delivery transform. These measurable
facts are hard gates. Composition, pacing, restraint, and cross-scene taste
remain Agent review judgments supported by decoded final-MP4 evidence.
