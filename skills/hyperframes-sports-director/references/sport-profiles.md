# Sport profiles

Choose exactly one profile from `profiles/sports/` and record its ID in the project. Profile policy shapes pacing and evidence use; it never authorizes invented footage, metrics, or unsafe interpretation.

| Profile | V1 maturity | Direction emphasis | Protected continuity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cycling` | Release-grade | route, effort, place | screen and motion direction; speech and road ambience |
| `hiking` | Release-grade | terrain, place, pauses | terrain/place continuity; speech, wind, and place ambience |
| `pool-swimming` | Release-grade | underwater color, lap rhythm, turns | lap/stroke continuity; splash and pool ambience |
| `running` | Experimental | cadence, effort, place | gait/screen direction; breath, speech, and footfall |
| `trail-running` | Experimental | terrain, cadence, effort | gait/terrain continuity; breath, footfall, and terrain ambience |
| `technical-mountaineering` | Experimental | terrain, altitude, risk context | risk/terrain continuity; safety calls, wind, and gear |
| `open-water-swimming` | Experimental | horizon, water color, stroke | horizon/stroke continuity; water ambience and breathing |

Hiking is the v1 non-technical mountain-journey profile. Do not silently treat technical mountaineering as hiking. Experimental profiles validate contract coverage but are not release-grade promises; state that boundary in the brief and delivery report.

Read the selected JSON for exact montage, stabilization, duplicate, audio, data, and visual policy. Pool swimming does not require or permit a GPS overlay. Other profiles permit recorded GPS but never require it. Missing activity data remains unavailable; it does not block a footage-led story.
